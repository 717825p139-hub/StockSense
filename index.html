The problem

Registers don't scale.
Spreadsheets don't reconcile.

  • Manual registers and disconnected spreadsheets
  • Separate records per warehouse, never in sync
  • Stock calculated by hand, error-prone
  • No clear trail of who moved what, and when
  • Low stock discovered too late — after it's gone

What it does

One ledger. Every movement.

Every receipt, delivery, transfer, and adjustment writes to the same stock ledger — so the number on screen always matches the shelf.

01

Authentication

Signup, login, and OTP-based password reset.

02

Dashboard

Live KPIs — stock levels, pending receipts, deliveries, transfers.

03

Product Management

Products, categories, units of measure, reordering rules.

04

Receipts

Log incoming stock from vendors — validate to update stock.

05

Delivery Orders

Pick, pack, and ship — stock decreases automatically.

06

Internal Transfers

Move stock between racks, floors, or warehouses.

07

Stock Adjustments

Reconcile system counts against physical counts.

08

Move History

A complete, timestamped ledger of every stock event.

09

Reports & Audit Logs

Inventory summaries plus a record of user and system activity.

10

Notifications

Low-stock and operational alerts, before they become problems.

11

Global Search

Find any product, order, or record in seconds.

12

AI Copilot

Ask questions about your inventory in plain language.
